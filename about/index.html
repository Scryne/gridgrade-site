    About GridGrade

    Electricity bills keep rising, but the numbers that explain them sit in government spreadsheets most people never open. GridGrade turns them into plain answers: what power costs in your state, who supplies your county, and how your utility compares on price and reliability.

    What makes it different

    • Utility-level, not just state averages. 1,328 utilities and 3,102 counties.
    • Graded, with the method in the open. Each report card shows its price and reliability sub-grades and links to the methodology.
    • Always current. The site rebuilds itself when the U.S. Energy Information Administration publishes new data.

    Independence

    GridGrade is independent. It is not affiliated with the U.S. Energy Information Administration, any utility or any electricity supplier, and it does not sell or rank anything for payment. The site may show advertising to cover its costs; ads never change the data or the grades.
