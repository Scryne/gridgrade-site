    Privacy policy

    Last updated October 24, 2026.

    What GridGrade collects

    Nothing directly. The site has no accounts, sign-ups, comments or contact forms, and the bill calculator runs entirely in your browser; the numbers you type are never sent anywhere.

    Third parties

    • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
    • Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when fonts are requested. See Google's privacy policy.
    • Advertising. GridGrade may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before such cookies are used.

    Changes

    Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
