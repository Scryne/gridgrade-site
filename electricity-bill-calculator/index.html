    How the estimate works

    Estimated bill = monthly kWh × average price per kWh. State averages divide all residential revenue by all residential sales, so they already include typical fixed charges spread over usage; a household that uses much less than average will usually pay a higher effective rate. Appliance figures are rounded typical values for planning only.
