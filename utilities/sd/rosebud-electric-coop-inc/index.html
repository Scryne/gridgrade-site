    How reliable is Rosebud Electric Coop Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Rosebud Electric Coop Inc customer was without power for 78 minutes across an average of 0.73 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in South Dakota is 64.9 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 141 minutes.
