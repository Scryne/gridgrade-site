    How reliable is City of Holyoke?

    In 2025 a typical City of Holyoke customer was without power for 37 minutes across an average of 0.46 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Massachusetts is 37.5 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 37 minutes.
