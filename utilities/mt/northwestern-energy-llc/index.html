    How reliable is NorthWestern Energy LLC?

    In 2025 a typical NorthWestern Energy LLC customer was without power for 117 minutes across an average of 1.06 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Montana is 160.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 125 minutes.
