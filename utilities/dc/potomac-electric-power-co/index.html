    How reliable is Potomac Electric Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Potomac Electric Power Co customer was without power for 34 minutes across an average of 0.31 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in District of Columbia is 33.9 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 50 minutes.
