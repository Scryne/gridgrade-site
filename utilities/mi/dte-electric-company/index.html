    How reliable is DTE Electric Company?

    In 2025 a typical DTE Electric Company customer was without power for 151 minutes across an average of 1.07 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Michigan is 142.0 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 192 minutes.
