    How reliable is Northern States Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Northern States Power Co customer was without power for 142 minutes across an average of 1.18 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Michigan is 142.0 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 738 minutes.
