    How reliable is Corridor Energy Cooperative?

    In 2025 a typical Corridor Energy Cooperative customer was without power for 135 minutes across an average of 1.77 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Iowa is 67.4 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 135 minutes.
