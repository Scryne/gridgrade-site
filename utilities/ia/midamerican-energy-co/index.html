    How reliable is MidAmerican Energy Co?

    In 2025 a typical MidAmerican Energy Co customer was without power for 82 minutes across an average of 0.75 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Iowa is 67.4 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 205 minutes.
