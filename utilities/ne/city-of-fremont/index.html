    How reliable is City of Fremont?

    In 2025 a typical City of Fremont customer was without power for 18 minutes across an average of 0.23 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Nebraska is 65.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 3081 minutes.
