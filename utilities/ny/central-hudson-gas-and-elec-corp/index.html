    How reliable is Central Hudson Gas & Elec Corp?

    In 2025 a typical Central Hudson Gas & Elec Corp customer was without power for 182 minutes across an average of 1.30 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in New York is 79.0 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 290 minutes.
