    How reliable is Niagara Mohawk Power Corp.?

    In 2025 a typical Niagara Mohawk Power Corp. customer was without power for 140 minutes across an average of 1.11 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in New York is 79.0 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 242 minutes.
