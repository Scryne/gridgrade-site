    How reliable is Tri-County Electric Coop, Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Tri-County Electric Coop, Inc customer was without power for 132 minutes across an average of 1.40 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Texas is 146.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 203 minutes.
