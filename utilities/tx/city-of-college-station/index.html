    How reliable is City of College Station?

    In 2025 a typical City of College Station customer was without power for 13 minutes across an average of 0.22 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Texas is 146.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 23 minutes.
