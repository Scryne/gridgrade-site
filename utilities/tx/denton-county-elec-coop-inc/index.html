    How reliable is Denton County Elec Coop, Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Denton County Elec Coop, Inc customer was without power for 37 minutes across an average of 0.29 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Texas is 146.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 49 minutes.
