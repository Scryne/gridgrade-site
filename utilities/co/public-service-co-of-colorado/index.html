    How reliable is Public Service Co of Colorado?

    In 2025 a typical Public Service Co of Colorado customer was without power for 149 minutes across an average of 1.36 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Colorado is 80.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 360 minutes.
