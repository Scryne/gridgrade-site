    How reliable is Otter Tail Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Otter Tail Power Co customer was without power for 132 minutes across an average of 1.33 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in North Dakota is 84.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 575 minutes.
