    How reliable is Northern Plains Electric Coop?

    In 2025 a typical Northern Plains Electric Coop customer was without power for 188 minutes across an average of 1.31 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in North Dakota is 84.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 371 minutes.
