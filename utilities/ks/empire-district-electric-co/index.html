    How reliable is Empire District Electric Co?

    In 2025 a typical Empire District Electric Co customer was without power for 91 minutes across an average of 1.17 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Kansas is 112.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 146 minutes.
