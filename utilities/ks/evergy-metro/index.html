    How reliable is Evergy Metro?

    In 2025 a typical Evergy Metro customer was without power for 64 minutes across an average of 0.73 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Kansas is 112.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 129 minutes.
