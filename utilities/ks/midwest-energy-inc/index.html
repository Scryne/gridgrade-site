    How reliable is Midwest Energy Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Midwest Energy Inc customer was without power for 314 minutes across an average of 2.23 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Kansas is 112.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 581 minutes.
