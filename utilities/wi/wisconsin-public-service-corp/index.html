    How reliable is Wisconsin Public Service Corp?

    In 2025 a typical Wisconsin Public Service Corp customer was without power for 107 minutes across an average of 0.86 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Wisconsin is 33.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 630 minutes.
