    How reliable is Wisconsin Electric Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Wisconsin Electric Power Co customer was without power for 93 minutes across an average of 0.66 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Wisconsin is 33.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 185 minutes.
