    How reliable is Polk-Burnett Electric Coop?

    In 2025 a typical Polk-Burnett Electric Coop customer was without power for 43 minutes across an average of 1.02 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Wisconsin is 33.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 104 minutes.
