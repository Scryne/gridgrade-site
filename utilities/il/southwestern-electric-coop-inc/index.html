    How reliable is Southwestern Electric Coop Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Southwestern Electric Coop Inc customer was without power for 194 minutes across an average of 1.68 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Illinois is 96.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 241 minutes.
