    How reliable is Rock Energy Cooperative?

    In 2025 a typical Rock Energy Cooperative customer was without power for 15 minutes across an average of 0.33 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Illinois is 96.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 16 minutes.
