    How reliable is Ameren Illinois Company?

    In 2025 a typical Ameren Illinois Company customer was without power for 130 minutes across an average of 0.94 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Illinois is 96.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 287 minutes.
