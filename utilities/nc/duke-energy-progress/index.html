    How reliable is Duke Energy Progress?

    In 2025 a typical Duke Energy Progress customer was without power for 145 minutes across an average of 1.26 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in North Carolina is 87.4 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 183 minutes.
