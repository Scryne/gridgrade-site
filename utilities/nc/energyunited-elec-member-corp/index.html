    How reliable is EnergyUnited Elec Member Corp?

    In 2025 a typical EnergyUnited Elec Member Corp customer was without power for 121 minutes across an average of 1.29 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in North Carolina is 87.4 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 169 minutes.
