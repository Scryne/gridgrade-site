    How reliable is Central Virginia Electric Coop?

    In 2025 a typical Central Virginia Electric Coop customer was without power for 501 minutes across an average of 3.47 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Virginia is 204.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 796 minutes.
