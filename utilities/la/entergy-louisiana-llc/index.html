    How reliable is Entergy Louisiana LLC?

    In 2025 a typical Entergy Louisiana LLC customer was without power for 218 minutes across an average of 1.66 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Louisiana is 228.5 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 253 minutes.
