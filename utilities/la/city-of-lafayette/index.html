    How reliable is City of Lafayette?

    In 2025 a typical City of Lafayette customer was without power for 27 minutes across an average of 0.64 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Louisiana is 228.5 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 30 minutes.
