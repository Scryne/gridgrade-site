    How reliable is Vermont Electric Cooperative, Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Vermont Electric Cooperative, Inc customer was without power for 151 minutes across an average of 1.45 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Vermont is 150.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 251 minutes.
