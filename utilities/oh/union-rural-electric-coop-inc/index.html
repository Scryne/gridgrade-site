    How reliable is Union Rural Electric Coop, Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Union Rural Electric Coop, Inc customer was without power for 62 minutes across an average of 1.01 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Ohio is 108.7 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 184 minutes.
