    How reliable is City of Hamilton?

    In 2025 a typical City of Hamilton customer was without power for 61 minutes across an average of 0.95 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Ohio is 108.7 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 121 minutes.
