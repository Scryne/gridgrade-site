    How reliable is Salt River Project?

    In 2025 a typical Salt River Project customer was without power for 50 minutes across an average of 0.89 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Arizona is 66.4 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 66 minutes.
