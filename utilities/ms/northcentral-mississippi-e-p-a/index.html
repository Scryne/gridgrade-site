    How reliable is Northcentral Mississippi E P A?

    In 2025 a typical Northcentral Mississippi E P A customer was without power for 245 minutes across an average of 1.52 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Mississippi is 200.7 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 245 minutes.
