    How reliable is Homer Electric Assn Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Homer Electric Assn Inc customer was without power for 239 minutes across an average of 1.75 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Alaska is 239.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 805 minutes.
