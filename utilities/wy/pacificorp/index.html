    How reliable is PacifiCorp?

    In 2025 a typical PacifiCorp customer was without power for 91 minutes across an average of 0.76 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Wyoming is 91.4 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 195 minutes.
