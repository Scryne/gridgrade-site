    How reliable is Inter County Energy Coop Corp?

    In 2025 a typical Inter County Energy Coop Corp customer was without power for 117 minutes across an average of 0.97 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Kentucky is 156.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 293 minutes.
