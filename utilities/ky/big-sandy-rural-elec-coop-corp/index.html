    How reliable is Big Sandy Rural Elec Coop Corp?

    In 2025 a typical Big Sandy Rural Elec Coop Corp customer was without power for 1138 minutes across an average of 4.91 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Kentucky is 156.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 1898 minutes.
