    How reliable is City of Frankfort?

    In 2025 a typical City of Frankfort customer was without power for 29 minutes across an average of 0.40 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Kentucky is 156.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 85 minutes.
