    How reliable is City of Idaho Falls?

    In 2025 a typical City of Idaho Falls customer was without power for 17 minutes across an average of 0.20 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Idaho is 118.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 51 minutes.
