    How reliable is Idaho Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Idaho Power Co customer was without power for 141 minutes across an average of 0.96 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Idaho is 118.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 171 minutes.
