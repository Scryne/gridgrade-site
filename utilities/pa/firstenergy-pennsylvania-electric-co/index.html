    How reliable is FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Electric Co?

    In 2025 a typical FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Electric Co customer was without power for 217 minutes across an average of 1.55 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Pennsylvania is 59.3 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 647 minutes.
