    How reliable is Borough of Chambersburg?

    In 2025 a typical Borough of Chambersburg customer was without power for 14 minutes across an average of 0.18 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Pennsylvania is 59.3 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 39 minutes.
