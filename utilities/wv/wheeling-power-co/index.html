    How reliable is Wheeling Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Wheeling Power Co customer was without power for 426 minutes across an average of 1.97 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in West Virginia is 485.9 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 706 minutes.
