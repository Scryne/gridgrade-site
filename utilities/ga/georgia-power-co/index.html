    How reliable is Georgia Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Georgia Power Co customer was without power for 155 minutes across an average of 1.44 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Georgia is 140.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 287 minutes.
