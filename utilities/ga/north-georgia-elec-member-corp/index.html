    How reliable is North Georgia Elec Member Corp?

    In 2025 a typical North Georgia Elec Member Corp customer was without power for 176 minutes across an average of 2.12 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Georgia is 140.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 215 minutes.
