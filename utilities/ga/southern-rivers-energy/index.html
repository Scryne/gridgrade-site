    How reliable is Southern Rivers Energy?

    In 2025 a typical Southern Rivers Energy customer was without power for 251 minutes across an average of 2.59 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Georgia is 140.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 774 minutes.
