    How reliable is Gulf Coast Electric Coop, Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Gulf Coast Electric Coop, Inc customer was without power for 257 minutes across an average of 2.66 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Florida is 149.4 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 336 minutes.
