    How reliable is Florida Power & Light Co?

    In 2025 a typical Florida Power & Light Co customer was without power for 45 minutes across an average of 0.43 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Florida is 149.4 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 47 minutes.
