    How reliable is Jersey Central Power & Lt Co?

    In 2025 a typical Jersey Central Power & Lt Co customer was without power for 234 minutes across an average of 1.89 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in New Jersey is 55.0 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 391 minutes.
