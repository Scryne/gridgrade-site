    How reliable is Central Maine Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Central Maine Power Co customer was without power for 177 minutes across an average of 1.74 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Maine is 223.0 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 318 minutes.
