    How reliable is Dakota Electric Association?

    In 2025 a typical Dakota Electric Association customer was without power for 20 minutes across an average of 0.29 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Minnesota is 69.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 29 minutes.
