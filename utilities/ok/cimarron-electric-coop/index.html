    How reliable is Cimarron Electric Coop?

    In 2025 a typical Cimarron Electric Coop customer was without power for 43 minutes across an average of 0.60 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Oklahoma is 124.8 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 70 minutes.
