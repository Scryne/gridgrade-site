    How reliable is East Central Oklahoma Elec Coop Inc?

    In 2025 a typical East Central Oklahoma Elec Coop Inc customer was without power for 321 minutes across an average of 2.70 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Oklahoma is 124.8 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 419 minutes.
