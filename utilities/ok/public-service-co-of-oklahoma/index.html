    How reliable is Public Service Co of Oklahoma?

    In 2025 a typical Public Service Co of Oklahoma customer was without power for 98 minutes across an average of 1.16 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Oklahoma is 124.8 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 200 minutes.
