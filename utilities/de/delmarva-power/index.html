    How reliable is Delmarva Power?

    In 2025 a typical Delmarva Power customer was without power for 76 minutes across an average of 0.69 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Delaware is 75.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 104 minutes.
