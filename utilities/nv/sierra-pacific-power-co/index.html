    How reliable is Sierra Pacific Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Sierra Pacific Power Co customer was without power for 240 minutes across an average of 2.27 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Nevada is 50.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 288 minutes.
