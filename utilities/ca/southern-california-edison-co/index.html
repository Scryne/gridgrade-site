    How reliable is Southern California Edison Co?

    In 2025 a typical Southern California Edison Co customer was without power for 116 minutes across an average of 0.96 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in California is 61.5 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 545 minutes.
