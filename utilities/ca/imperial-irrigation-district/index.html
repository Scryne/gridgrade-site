    How reliable is Imperial Irrigation District?

    In 2025 a typical Imperial Irrigation District customer was without power for 56 minutes across an average of 0.66 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in California is 61.5 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 114 minutes.
