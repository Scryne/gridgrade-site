    How reliable is Easton Utilities Comm?

    In 2025 a typical Easton Utilities Comm customer was without power for 21 minutes across an average of 0.42 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Maryland is 66.0 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 30 minutes.
