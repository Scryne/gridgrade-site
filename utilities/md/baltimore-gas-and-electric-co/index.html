    How reliable is Baltimore Gas & Electric Co?

    In 2025 a typical Baltimore Gas & Electric Co customer was without power for 65 minutes across an average of 0.64 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Maryland is 66.0 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 175 minutes.
