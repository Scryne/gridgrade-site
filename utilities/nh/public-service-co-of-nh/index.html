    How reliable is Public Service Co of NH?

    In 2025 a typical Public Service Co of NH customer was without power for 83 minutes across an average of 0.74 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in New Hampshire is 91.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 160 minutes.
