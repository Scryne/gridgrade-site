    How reliable is City of Paris?

    In 2025 a typical City of Paris customer was without power for 444 minutes across an average of 4.82 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Tennessee is 134.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 488 minutes.
