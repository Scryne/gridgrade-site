    How reliable is Fort Loudoun Electric Coop?

    In 2025 a typical Fort Loudoun Electric Coop customer was without power for 433 minutes across an average of 2.35 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Tennessee is 134.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 530 minutes.
