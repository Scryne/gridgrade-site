    In 2025 a typical Coosa Valley Electric Coop Inc customer was without power for 209 minutes across an average of 1.84 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Alabama is 106.2 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 345 minutes.
