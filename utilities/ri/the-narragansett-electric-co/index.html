    How reliable is The Narragansett Electric Co?

    In 2025 a typical The Narragansett Electric Co customer was without power for 60 minutes across an average of 0.73 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Rhode Island is 60.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 183 minutes.
