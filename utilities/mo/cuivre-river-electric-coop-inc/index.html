    In 2025 a typical Cuivre River Electric Coop Inc customer was without power for 135 minutes across an average of 1.21 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Missouri is 104.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 135 minutes.
