    How reliable is City of Carthage?

    In 2025 a typical City of Carthage customer was without power for 37 minutes across an average of 1.07 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Missouri is 104.1 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 321 minutes.
