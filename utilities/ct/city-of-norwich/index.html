    How reliable is City of Norwich?

    In 2025 a typical City of Norwich customer was without power for 169 minutes across an average of 1.48 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Connecticut is 78.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 169 minutes.
