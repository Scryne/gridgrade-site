    How reliable is Connecticut Light & Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Connecticut Light & Power Co customer was without power for 85 minutes across an average of 0.69 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Connecticut is 78.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 217 minutes.
