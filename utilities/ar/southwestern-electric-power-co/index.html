    How reliable is Southwestern Electric Power Co?

    In 2025 a typical Southwestern Electric Power Co customer was without power for 212 minutes across an average of 1.65 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Arkansas is 192.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 428 minutes.
