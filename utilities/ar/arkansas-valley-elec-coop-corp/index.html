    How reliable is Arkansas Valley Elec Coop Corp?

    In 2025 a typical Arkansas Valley Elec Coop Corp customer was without power for 249 minutes across an average of 2.59 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Arkansas is 192.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 322 minutes.
