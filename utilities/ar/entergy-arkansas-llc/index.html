    How reliable is Entergy Arkansas LLC?

    In 2025 a typical Entergy Arkansas LLC customer was without power for 252 minutes across an average of 1.63 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Arkansas is 192.6 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 518 minutes.
