    How reliable is Decatur County Rural E M C?

    In 2025 a typical Decatur County Rural E M C customer was without power for 220 minutes across an average of 2.03 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Indiana is 140.9 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 463 minutes.
