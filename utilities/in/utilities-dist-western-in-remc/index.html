    How reliable is Utilities Dist-Western IN REMC?

    In 2025 a typical Utilities Dist-Western IN REMC customer was without power for 363 minutes across an average of 2.58 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in Indiana is 140.9 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 763 minutes.
