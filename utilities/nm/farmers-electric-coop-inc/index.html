    How reliable is Farmers Electric Coop, Inc?

    In 2025 a typical Farmers Electric Coop, Inc customer was without power for 198 minutes across an average of 1.53 outages, excluding major storms (IEEE 1366 "major event days"). The US utility median is 103.3 minutes, and the median in New Mexico is 171.5 minutes. Including major storms, outages totaled 198 minutes.
