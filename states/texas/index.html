    Electricity rates in Texas

    15.91¢per kWh, residential

    Households in Texas paid an average of 15.91¢/kWh in August 2026, 12% below the US average of 18.16¢. That is up 3.3% from August 2025. Texas ranks #22 of 51 from cheapest to most expensive.

    Where Texas's electricity comes from

    In 2025, natural gas generated the most electricity in Texas, 48.2% of the total. Wind, solar and hydro together supplied 32.7% (US: 22.6%). Coal's share fell from 27.0% in 2015 to 11.4%.

    About these numbers

    Monthly prices are average revenue per kWh (bills divided by usage) reported to the U.S. Energy Information Administration. Per EIA's reporting rules they include customer charges, energy and demand charges, fuel adjustments, environmental surcharges and franchise fees; sales taxes added separately to your bill may not be included. Your rate depends on your utility, plan and usage. Estimate your bill or read the methodology.
