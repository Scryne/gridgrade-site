    Electricity rates in Washington

    14.63¢per kWh, residential

    Households in Washington paid an average of 14.63¢/kWh in August 2026, 19% below the US average of 18.16¢. That is up 7.2% from August 2025. Washington ranks #12 of 51 from cheapest to most expensive.

    Where Washington's electricity comes from

    In 2025, hydro generated the most electricity in Washington, 63.6% of the total. Wind, solar and hydro together supplied 72.1% (US: 22.6%).

    About these numbers

    Monthly prices are average revenue per kWh (bills divided by usage) reported to the U.S. Energy Information Administration. Per EIA's reporting rules they include customer charges, energy and demand charges, fuel adjustments, environmental surcharges and franchise fees; sales taxes added separately to your bill may not be included. Your rate depends on your utility, plan and usage. Estimate your bill or read the methodology.
